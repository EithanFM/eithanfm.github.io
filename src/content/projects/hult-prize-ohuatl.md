## Context
From September 2025 to February 2026 I participated in the Hult Prize at Hult International Business School. Our venture concept, Ohuatl, addressed textile waste through a circular fashion solution aligned with UN SDGs 12 and 13.

## Question or thesis
Given the scale of global textile waste (UN SDG-aligned problem framing), is there a viable circular fashion model for the Mexican market, and what demand and growth evidence support it?

## Method
I co-developed Ohuatl as a circular fashion solution and helped validate the opportunity with market research focused on Mexico. The work covered industry growth, consumer interest, and market size trajectories.

## Result
We validated industry growth, consumer interest in Mexico, and a multi-year market-size trajectory using primary and secondary research. That evidence framed the venture case for the Hult Prize process.

## Skills and tools
Market research; sustainability and circular economy framing; venture pitch development; PowerPoint; teamwork.
