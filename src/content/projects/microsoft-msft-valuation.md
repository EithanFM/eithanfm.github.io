Title: Microsoft (MSFT) Equity Research & Valuation
Dates: Sep to Dec 2025
Hult project: YES (FIN-0300 Corporate Finance course project)
Publishable files: none

## Context
This was my Corporate Finance (FIN-0300) equity research project at Hult International Business School. I valued Microsoft (MSFT) as a student analyst building a full investment case from public filings and market data.

Academic project, not investment advice.

## Question or thesis
Is MSFT fairly valued at the then current market price, and what target price and recommendation follow from a disciplined DCF plus comps framework?

## Method
I built a three-stage discounted cash flow model in Excel: forecasted the financial statements, derived WACC, and ran sensitivity analysis alongside comparable company multiples. The investment case also covered subscription revenue drivers, the AI and Copilot competitive edge, a risk matrix, and an ESG review.

## Result
I delivered a formal SELL recommendation with a $134.57 target price, implying about 72% downside from $485.50 at the time of the analysis. The conclusion rested on the DCF base case, supported by the qualitative drivers and risk work above.

## Skills and tools
Excel financial modeling (DCF, WACC, sensitivity analysis, comps); PowerPoint; Word; public company filings and market data.
