## Context
In April 2026 I competed in the Korn Ferry Client Case Competition at Hult (STR-0200). Our five-person team proposed an AI client intelligence and performance management platform to a judging panel.

## Question or thesis
How should a professional services firm implement an AI client-intelligence platform in phases, with governance that keeps humans in control?

## Method
I built the three-phase implementation roadmap: CRM data integration; AI dashboards and recommendations; global scale-up. The roadmap included governance controls for secure access, human review, and phased rollout. I presented the conclusion and the business case to the judging panel.

## Result
We delivered a complete platform proposal and implementation roadmap to the judges. I owned the roadmap and the closing business case.

## Skills and tools
Implementation roadmapping; AI product framing for consulting clients; governance design; PowerPoint; team case presentation.
