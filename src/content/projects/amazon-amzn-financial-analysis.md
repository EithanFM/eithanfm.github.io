Title: Amazon (AMZN) Financial Analysis 2020 to 2025
Dates: Feb to Apr 2025
Hult project: YES (FIN-1, individual coursework)
Publishable files: none

## Context
From February to April 2025 I completed an individual financial analysis of Amazon (AMZN) covering 2020 to 2025 as FIN-1 coursework at Hult International Business School. The project used public SEC filings as the primary source.

Academic project, not investment advice.

## Question or thesis
How did Amazon's financial position and performance evolve over six years, and what growth trends follow from that history?

## Method
I analyzed six years of SEC filings across revenue, net income, assets, liabilities, and equity. I organized the series into growth trends and prepared recommendations for presentation.

## Result
I presented the growth trends and recommendations based on the six-year filing review.

## Skills and tools
SEC filing analysis; financial statement review; Excel; presentation of trends and recommendations.
