## Context
In August 2025 I took part in the CME Group Derivatives Trading Challenge as part of FIN-C (Derivatives & Risk Management) at Hult International Business School, using the CME Group trading simulation. The brief was to design and execute a futures strategy and present it to a judging panel.

## Question or thesis
Can a rules-based, trend-following approach on a liquid equity-index future produce a clear, measurable result within the challenge window?

## Method
I built and executed a trend-following futures strategy focused on Russell 2000 (RTY) futures. The strategy was presented to the judging panel as part of the challenge.

## Result
The Russell 2000 (RTY) futures trade returned +3.49% ROI.

## Skills and tools
Futures market concepts; trend-following strategy design; trade execution and presentation for a judging panel.
