## projectPage.global-crises-ai-minerals.section.Method
EN: Method
ES:

## projectPage.global-crises-ai-minerals.Method.paragraph.1
EN: As a team we mapped the midstream segment from mining through refining to chips, hardware, and data centers, identified import dependence on Chinese refining as the primary vulnerability, traced transmission pathways from a Hormuz disruption, and outlined an early-warning dashboard.
